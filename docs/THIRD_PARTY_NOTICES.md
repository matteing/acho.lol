- **Bricolage Grotesque and Instrument Sans** are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete licenses are in `public/fonts/licenses/` and `scripts/assets/fonts/`. The static build-only derivatives use the renamed families Acho Social Display and Acho Social Text; see [the font-generation notes](../scripts/SOCIAL-CARDS.md).

- **External embeds** remain hosted by their providers and are not distributed as local media.

When adding content or assets, preserve attribution and document the applicable license. Do not assume that a publicly viewable asset is freely licensed.

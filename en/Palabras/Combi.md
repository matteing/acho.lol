## Meaning

Short for *la combinación* (“the combination”), *combi* generally describes a set of things that belong together. Its exact meaning depends on context.

### 1. Puerto Rican Chinese food

The standard meal format at Chinese restaurants in Puerto Rico: a massive plate with a protein, Puerto Rican-style fried rice, and a side—usually french fries or tostones.

1. **Protein:** pepper steak, sweet-and-sour chicken, spare ribs, and so on.
2. **Rice:** yellow-orange Puerto Rican-style fried rice, often with ham.
3. **Side:** french fries or tostones.

It is a culturally iconic meal.

### 2. In reggaetón

*La combi completa* describes someone—usually a woman—who has “the whole package,” meaning every physically desirable quality.

The phrase reportedly grew out of a conversation between Nicky Jam and Daddy Yankee in Puerto Rico around 1999. Nicky told a woman *yo quiero la combi completa* (“I want the complete combo”); the phrase became a hook and then an iconic hit.

## Closest English equivalent

“The combo,” though the Puerto Rican Chinese-food *combi* is culturally distinct.

## Usage

- *Dame una combi de pepper steak con tostones.* — Give me a pepper-steak combo with tostones.
- *Quiero una combi china.* — I want a Chinese combo.
- *Esa jeva es la combi completa.* — That woman is the complete package.

## Variants

- *La combi.*
- *La combi completa* — the complete package; also metaphorical.
- *Sin arroz* — without rice; rarely requested.

## Origin

The Chinese-food meaning probably emerged as local Chinese restaurants adapted their menus to Puerto Rican tastes by adding tostones, french fries, and local-style yellow fried rice.

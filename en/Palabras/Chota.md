## Meaning

A **chota** is an informant: a snitch or rat who cooperates with police or other authorities. On the street this is not a neutral description. It is an accusation of betrayal and of breaking [[en/Palabras/Códigos|*los códigos*]].

> [!WARNING] Do not casually call someone *chota*
> It is an extremely serious accusation and a powerful insult in street contexts. Calling the wrong person a *chota* can provoke violence.

## Closest English equivalents

“Snitch,” “informant,” or “rat.”

## Usage

- *Nadie quiere ser un chota.* — Nobody wants to be a snitch.
- *Cuidado con ese cabrón, que es chota.* — Watch out for that guy; he is a snitch.
- *No le cuentes na’ a ese tipo, que él chotea.* — Do not tell that guy anything; he informs.
- *Los chotas no duran en la calle.* — Snitches do not last in the street.

## Variants

- **Chota / chotas** — singular and plural.
- **Chotear** — to inform on someone or give information to authorities.
- **Choteo** — the act of informing.

## Related terms

- **Sapo** — another word for an informant or snitch.
- **Bocón** — someone who talks too much; not necessarily a *chota*.
- **Tirar al medio** — to expose someone or throw them under the bus.

## Cultural context

In Puerto Rican street culture, *chota* is one of the worst accusations someone can make. It implies betrayal, cooperation with law enforcement, and total loss of credibility. In less intense settings—among children, at school, or among friends—it may also describe someone who tells on others to a teacher, parent, or authority figure.

Not every *bocón* is a *chota*, but every *chota* said too much.

## In music and *maleanteo*

Puerto Rican trap and reggaetón use *chota* constantly to attack an artist’s [[en/Palabras/Códigos|code]], loyalty, and street credibility. The accusation is a recurring weapon in *tiraeras* and [[en/Palabras/Maleante|*maleanteo*]].

### Anuel AA — *Arcángel es chota*

The title leaves little room for interpretation. Anuel turns the accusation into the central concept of a ten-minute diss track.

### The accusation against YOVNGCHIMI

YOVNGCHIMI has also been targeted by the label in diss tracks, edits, and third-party videos. Repetition can damage an artist’s street image even when it does not establish that the artist cooperated with authorities.

### Anuel AA — *Nunca sapo*

Here **sapo** works as a synonym for *chota*. *Siempre leal, nunca sapo*—“always loyal, never a snitch”—summarizes the loyalty ideal behind the street code.

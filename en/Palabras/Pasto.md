Marijuana.

*Pasto* and *mota* are basically synonyms in Puerto Rico. They are used interchangeably or together as a pair—*pasto y mota*—to refer to weed.

> “Marihuana, pasto, perico, fentanilo, capea lo tuyo hoy.”
>
> — Alexandra Lúgaro

**Pasto** literally means grass. **Mota** (or the diminutive *motita*) is direct slang for marijuana.

## Closest English equivalents

- “Grass,” “weed,” or “pot.”
- *Pasto y mota* literally pairs two words for weed, like saying “weed and grass.”

## Usage

- *Vamos a fumar pasto.* — Let’s smoke weed.
- *¿Dónde consigo mota?* — Where can I get weed?
- *Ese tipo siempre está en pasto y mota.* — That guy is always into weed.
- *Huele a pasto aquí, [[en/Palabras/Cabrón|cabrón]].* — It smells like weed in here, dude.

## Variants

- *Pasto* — grass; weed.
- *Mota* — weed.
- *Pasto y mota* — the pair, often said together.
- *Hierba / yerba* — another common synonym.
- *Tronco* — the joint or blunt itself.

## Related terms

- *Fumeta* — someone who smokes regularly; a pothead.
- *Fumeteo* — the activity of smoking.
- *Ganja* — cannabis; a more widely recognized term.

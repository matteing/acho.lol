*Los códigos*—literally “the codes”—can mean several things in Puerto Rico.

### 1. The street code

The rules of conduct, loyalty, and silence in the street. Breaking *los códigos* by becoming a [[en/Palabras/Chota|*chota*]], informing, or betraying someone is the ultimate offense. A real [[en/Palabras/Maleante|*maleante*]] lives by the code.

### 2. “Cheat codes”

More generally, *los códigos* can be a secret, trick, or piece of insider information that gives you an advantage: video-game cheat codes for real life. A friend “putting you on” to something is giving you *los códigos*.

## Closest English equivalents

- “The code” or “street code” in street contexts.
- “The cheat codes,” “inside knowledge,” or “the secret sauce” more generally.

## Usage

Street context:

> *Él no respeta los códigos, por eso nadie confía en él.* — He does not respect the code, so nobody trusts him.

> *Cuando vayas a La Perla, tienes que respetar los códigos, brodel.* — When you go to La Perla, you have to respect the code, brother.

General context:

> *Ah, diablo, bro, esos son códigos.* — Damn, bro, that is insider knowledge.

> *Pásame los códigos para ese website de películas.* — Send me the hookup for that movie website.

## Origin and context

The term exploded in popularity through reggaetón, trap, and [[en/Palabras/Maleante|*maleanteo*]], where hierarchy and loyalty are constant themes. From there it jumped into everyday speech for any winning strategy or piece of insider knowledge.

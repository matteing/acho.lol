## Meaning

An ugly mess or chaotic situation, normally with a vulgar tone.

## Closest English equivalents

“A shitshow” or “a complete mess.”

## Usage

- *Esto está en un despingue.* — This is a complete mess.
- *Se formó un despingue en la cancha.* — Chaos broke out on the court.
- *Con la música y la gritadera, esto es un despingue.* — With the music and all the yelling, this is a shitshow.

## Variant

- *Está hecho un despingue.* — It has become a complete mess.

## Meaning

### 1. A mess or problem (slang)

A colloquial and mildly vulgar way to say that there is a problem, conflict, or complicated situation.

### 2. Flatulence (literal)

A *peo* is also literally intestinal gas—a [[en/Palabras/Follón|*follón*]].

> [!TIP] How can you tell which meaning applies?
> Context does all the work. *Eso es un jodío peo* means “that is a damn problem.”
> *Me tiré un peo* means “I farted.”

## Closest English equivalents

- Sense 1: “a mess” or “a problem.”
- Sense 2: “a fart.”

## Usage

- *Aquí hay un peo.* — There is a problem here.
- *Se formó un peo con la reservación.* — The reservation became a whole mess.
- *Eso va a ser un peo para resolver.* — That is going to be a pain to solve.
- *Me tiré un peo.* — I farted.

## Variant

- *Qué peo.* — What a mess.

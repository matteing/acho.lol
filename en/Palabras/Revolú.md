## Meaning

A commotion, noisy disorder, or chaos. It is extremely common and not necessarily vulgar.

## Closest English equivalents

“Commotion,” “a mess,” or “a whole situation.”

## Usage

- *Qué revolú hay aquí.* — What a commotion in here.
- *Con la lluvia, se formó un revolú en la carretera.* — The rain caused a mess on the road.
- *No armes revolú.* — Do not start a commotion.

## Variants

- *Revolú* / *revolú*.

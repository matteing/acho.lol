## Meaning

A major problem, a mess, or a deeply inconvenient situation. It sounds vulgar.

## Closest English equivalents

“A pain in the ass” or, depending on context, “a shitshow.”

## Usage

- *Esto es una jodienda.* — This is a pain in the ass.
- *Se formó una jodienda con el vuelo.* — The flight situation became a huge mess.
- *Resolver eso es una jodienda.* — Sorting that out is a pain in the ass.

## Variant

- *Qué jodienda.* — What a pain.

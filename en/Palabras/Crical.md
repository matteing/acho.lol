## Meaning

An intense mess or chaos. It can also suggest conflict or a situation that has spun out of control.

## Closest English equivalents

“A big mess” or “a shitshow.”

## Usage

- *Aquí hay un crical.* — It is chaos in here.
- *Se formó un crical en la entrada.* — A huge mess broke out at the entrance.
- *Con tanta gente gritando, esto es un crical.* — With so many people yelling, this is a shitshow.

## Variants

- *Un crical.*
- *Se formó un crical.* — A *crical* broke out.

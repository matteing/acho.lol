Fabio is the celebrated writer of *911*, a popular song by urban artist **Mora**.

Joking aside: on Puerto Rican Twitter—back when it was still called Twitter, RIP—a Mora fan asked who had written Jhayco’s famous verse on *911*. The question appeared during a *tiraera* following the release of Ankhal’s song *SI PEPE*.

Mora replied: **“Fabio.”** Think of it as one of Puerto Rico’s many equivalents to a “deez nuts” joke.

The unfortunate fan then asked *¿quién carajo es Fabio?* (“who the hell is Fabio?”), triggering a catastrophic series of events in Mora’s mind. There was no going back.

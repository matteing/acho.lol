Just as Puerto Rican Spanish has many names for [[en/Artículos/Las miles de maneras de dar un golpe|individual slaps and blows]], it offers several ways to describe overpowering or beating someone.

These expressions imply more than one isolated hit and may also describe a decisive victory by a large margin:

- *Una pela* — a beating.
- *Caerle encima a alguien* — to descend on or overwhelm someone.
- *Dar una catimba* — to give someone a thorough beating.
- *Darle como pillo de película* — to beat someone like a movie villain.

## Usage

- *Le dieron una catimba.* — They gave him a beating.
- *Te van a dar una catimba si sigues.* — They will beat you if you keep going.
- *Se ganaron una catimba bien dada.* — They earned a proper beating.

## Related expressions

- [[en/Artículos/Las miles de maneras de dar un golpe|The thousand ways to hit someone]].
- *Una galleta* — a slap.
- *Una pela* — a beating.
- *Darle como pillo de película* — to beat someone up dramatically.

## Origin note

The origin of *catimba* is uncertain. The term is used throughout Puerto Rico for a thorough beating.

The purpose of acho.lol is to **preserve Puerto Rican popular culture**.

No pretentiousness. We document things, people, sayings, slang—and yes, even gossip. This is not meant to be a bourgeois or elitist project. We want to collect **every** layer of Boricua life, from the most ordinary and informal to the most serious and illustrious.

Too much of our culture goes unpreserved because people fear looking [[en/Artículos/¿Quién es Fabio?|*cafre*]] or feel compelled to sanitize culture until it looks presentable. This wiki exists to do the opposite.

The general tone is [[en/Controversias/A real world account of Caguas|informal and playful]], because popular culture often works that way. That does not prevent us from covering serious subjects. The mission is ultimately to **share, preserve, and teach** Puerto Rican culture and history—especially modern history—in an accessible way.

We may live on a small island, but the culture is enormous: countless regionalisms, variants, words, and [[en/Artículos/Las miles de maneras de dar una golpiza|ways to express the same idea]]. We want them documented.

Informal does not mean careless.

- Information should be as **accurate** as possible.
- Claims that cannot be verified must be clearly marked, for example with a warning callout.

And no, this is not trying to become an academic encyclopedia written by formal historians. There are far too many memes here for that.

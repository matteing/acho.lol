People from Caguas (*cagüeños*) and Ponce (*ponceños*) are the traditional contenders.

Ponceños simply believe they are *la última Coca-Cola del desierto*—literally “the last Coca-Cola in the desert,” or the greatest and most desirable thing around. *Ponce es Ponce y el resto es parking* (“Ponce is Ponce and the rest is parking”) is a common slogan.

Cagüeños, meanwhile, are widely treated as a red flag. They are supposedly toxic and believe Caguas is [[en/Controversias/A real world account of Caguas|far more impressive than it really is]].

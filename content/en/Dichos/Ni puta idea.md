## Meaning

A vulgar way to say that you do not have the slightest idea. It is often abbreviated **NPI**.

## Closest English equivalents

“No fucking clue” or “NFI” (“no fucking idea”).

## Usage

- *¿Qué hora es? — Ni puta idea, cabrón.* — What time is it? No fucking clue, man.
- *Eso lo sabe él; yo no tengo ni puta idea.* — He knows that; I have no fucking idea.

## Variants

- *NPI* — abbreviation.
- *Ni la más mínima idea* — polite/formal version: “not the slightest idea.”

## Origin

Obvio.

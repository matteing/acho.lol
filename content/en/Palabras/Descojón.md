## Meaning

A very vulgar way to say that something is complete chaos or an enormous mess.

## Closest English equivalent

“A total shitshow.”

## Usage

- *Aquí hay un descojón.* — It is a total shitshow in here.
- *Se formó un descojón con el sistema.* — The system turned into a complete mess.
- *Con tanta gente y cero organización, esto es un descojón.* — With so many people and zero organization, this is a total shitshow.

## Meaning

Intestinal gas; a synonym for [[en/Palabras/Peo|*peo*]] in its literal sense.

## English equivalent

“A fart.”

## Usage

- *Se tiró un follón.* — They farted.
- *Huele a follón.* — It smells like a fart.

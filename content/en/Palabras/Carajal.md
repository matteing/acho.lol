## Meaning

A major mess or chaotic situation, usually with a vulgar tone—something like a *tremendo desorden* (“one hell of a mess”).

## Closest English equivalents

“A shitshow” or “a total mess.”

## Usage

- *Esto es un carajal.* — This is a total mess.
- *Se formó un carajal en la oficina.* — A huge mess broke out at the office.
- *Con tanto reguero, aquí hay un carajal.* — With all this disorder, this place is a shitshow.

## Variants

- *Un carajal.*
